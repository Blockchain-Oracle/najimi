A workflow is a recipe over the same tools the chat always has. It runs in a chat, for you or anyone in People, and you can talk to it as it goes.

## The built-ins

| Workflow | Needs | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relight my room** | a photo | Asks what the room is for and the mood you want, restyles your room in two lighting moods (keeping the room exactly), writes a lighting plan an electrician can install, finds real lamps from brands that fit, and picks the room's soundtrack. |
| **Make it mine** | a photo | Restyles anything you photograph (a desk, a shelf, a cup, an outfit) in your taste, with real products. |
| **Cook for…** | someone | Learns their diet, allergies, texture level and medications; reads the FDA label first; then a recipe at their texture and temperature, a photo of the dish, and a week's plan with a shopping list. |
| **Next read** | | Books from your taste, the world of the top pick as a still, a reading playlist, and a place to read it. |
| **Dress me** | | An outfit from your style words, real pieces from brands that fit, and music for getting ready. |
| **My taste film** | | A cinematic still made of your words, then a short film with sound, and a soundtrack. |
| **Night in** | | Who it's for and the mood, then a film, music, takeaway near you, or a recipe. |

No photo? Every photo workflow offers three sample rooms.

## Links

Every workflow has its own page at `/w/<name>`, with a picture for chat apps. Send it to anyone: someone new tells Najimi three things they love, sees their taste, and carries straight on into the workflow.

## Your own

When a chat goes well, **Save as a workflow** (under the last answer). Najimi drafts the steps from the chat in your language, written the way you'd ask, with no names or one-off details. Edit them and save.

- Your workflows sit under **Yours** on the Workflows page: run, delete, or switch on **Anyone with the link can run it** to get a `/w/` link.
- A saved workflow runs by sending its steps as *your* first message. It never becomes instructions to the model, so a shared workflow can't change how Najimi behaves.
