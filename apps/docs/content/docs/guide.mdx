## Your taste

**Start** asks for a face and a name, a city (optional, for places near you), and three or more things you love. Search shows real Qloo artwork as you type. Then say how much each one counts: a love at 9 pulls harder than one at 3.

The **reveal** happens live. Your taste words land first, in families:

| Family | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | Aesthetic words | Minimalist, Clean Lines |
| Rooms | Decor | Homey, Speakeasy |
| Mood | Ambience | Warm, Chill |
| Voice | Style of the media you love | Narratively Fragmented |
| Food | Cuisines and dishes | Caribbean, Comfort Food |

Then picks arrive domain by domain. Each pick has a **because** chip naming the love that led to it. On a pick you can **Keep** it, ask for **More like this**, or say **Not for me** (it never comes back).

## The chat

Ask Najimi anything about your taste: who you are, where in a city your people are, what to watch, read or play next, hidden gems, a trip. The agent composes Qloo's tools for each ask, and every step it takes is shown above its answer.

Answers come as cards: picks of any kind, taste words, a map of where people with your taste go in a city, an age and gender lean, and a comparison of two tastes. Every Qloo card has a **Qloo** chip that opens the exact request behind it.

The composer takes words, your voice (tap the mic) and photos: Najimi can restyle a photo of a room to your taste. *Ask first* shows the cost before anything is made; *Make automatically* goes straight ahead; *Plan only* describes and never makes.

## Sharing

A taste or a chat can be shared as a link that unfurls with a picture. Your own photos are never shown. **Settings → Your share links** lists them all; **Stop sharing** ends a link at once.

## Languages

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic (right to left), plus Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, Hindi, Chinese and Japanese in beta.

## Settings

Language, the default mode for new chats, today's allowance, your share links, your account, and **Delete all my data**.
