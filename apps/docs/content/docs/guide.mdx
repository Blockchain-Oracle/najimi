## Your taste

**Start** asks for a name, a city (optional, for places near you), and three or more things you love. Search shows real Qloo artwork as you type. Then say how much each one counts: a love at 9 pulls harder than one at 3.

The **reveal** happens live. Your taste words land first, in families:

| Family | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | Aesthetic words | Minimalist, Clean Lines |
| Rooms | Decor | Homey, Speakeasy |
| Mood | Ambience | Warm, Chill |
| Voice | Style of the media you love | Narratively Fragmented |
| Food | Cuisines and dishes | Caribbean, Comfort Food |

Then picks arrive domain by domain (music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places and more). Each pick has a **because** chip naming the love that led to it. **What your taste connects** shows the links across domains ("Insecure led to Jorja Smith").

On a pick you can **Keep** it, ask for **More like this**, or say **Not for me** (it never comes back).

## People you make for

**People** holds a taste for anyone: Mum, a partner, a client. Each has their own loves and their own **About**: diet, allergies, texture level (IDDSI 3–7), serving temperature, budget and medications. Najimi uses About only when making things *for that person*, and never shares it.

You can also tell the chat "my grandmother loves Fela and jollof": it saves her as a person on its own.

## The chat

Ask anything. The composer has:

- **Who it's for:** you, or anyone in People.
- **Mode**, for anything that costs (images, video): *Ask first* shows what it will make and the cost before making it; *Make automatically* goes straight ahead; *Plan only* describes and never makes.
- **Photos:** add your own, or pick a sample room.
- **Voice:** tap the mic, talk, and the words land in the box to read before sending.
- **Workflows:** start a ready-made recipe.

Answers come as cards: picks, taste words, a map of where people with your taste go in a city, an age and gender lean, a comparison of two tastes, and the things Najimi made. Every Qloo card has a **Qloo** chip that opens the exact request behind it.

Under the last answer: copy, regenerate, fork the chat from there, share it, **save it as a workflow**, and **Compare without taste**.

### Compare without taste

The same ask, answered with no taste and no Qloo, beside Najimi's answer. The difference is what Qloo adds.

## What Najimi makes

| Ask | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| "Relight my room" + a photo | Two restyles of *your* room (same walls, same furniture), and a lighting plan: layers, fixtures, bulbs in kelvin and lumens, placement, an electrician's checklist. |
| "Find me lamps that fit" | Real products from brands Qloo matches to your taste, read from each brand's own site, with prices. |
| "Cook for Mum" | A recipe from her cuisines at her texture and temperature, a photo of the dish, a week's plan with a shopping list, and notes from her medication's FDA label (vitamin K with warfarin, grapefruit with some statins). |
| "Make me a short film of my taste" | A still built from your words, then a slow camera move with sound. |
| "A playlist for a rainy Sunday" | Artists from your taste with a mood tag from Qloo. |

Images and videos count against a daily allowance; **Settings** shows what's left.

## Sharing

- **A creation:** the result only. Your own photo is never shown.
- **A chat:** read-only, with its cards and results. Your photos are removed before the page is built.
- **A taste:** the loves, words, connections and picks. Never the city, age or anything in About.

Every link unfurls with a picture in chat apps. **Settings → Your share links** lists them all; **Stop sharing** ends a link, its media and its preview at once.

## Languages

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic (right to left), plus Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, Hindi, Chinese and Japanese in beta. The chat answers in the language you chose.

## Settings

Language, the default mode for new chats, today's allowance, your share links, your account, and **Delete all my data**.
