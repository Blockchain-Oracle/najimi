---
title: Using Najimi
description: Every screen, tab by tab.
---

Najimi has five tabs: **Feed**, **People**, **Najimi** (the agent, in the middle), **Earth** and **You**. The agent is also one tap away on every screen, and it knows what you're looking at.

## Start

Pick a face and a name, a city if you like, and three or more things you love. Search shows real Qloo artwork as you type. You can also bring your taste in by name: a Letterboxd, Last.fm or Goodreads username, or a list of X and Instagram handles. Your taste builds live, and you choose a username at the end.

## Feed

A full-screen feed of culture from your taste: music with 30-second previews, film and TV trailers, podcast episodes, books, places and brands. Every card says why it's there (*because you love Fleabag*).

- **Love** a card and it becomes part of your taste. **Skip** and it won't come back.
- **Tune** the mix: more music, less film, nothing from podcasts. The feed reshapes at once.
- One player follows you around the app, with lock-screen controls.

## People

- **Search** by username, follow people, and see your **match** with anyone (Qloo compares your tastes).
- **Taste twins**: the people here whose taste is closest to yours.
- **Circles**: group chats with Najimi in them. Invite by link or QR code. Mention `@najimi` and it answers for the whole room, by name.
- **Games**: *Guess my wavelength*, *How well do you know me*, *Taste twins* and *Blind Taste*. Qloo knows the answers; Najimi plays too. Every circle gets a daily drop, and there's a weekly leaderboard. Challenge links work without an account.

## Najimi

Your own chat with the agent. Ask anything about your taste, or ask it to do something: plan a night, find a hotel, call a restaurant, start a game, tune your feed, draft a post, build your business plan. See [Najimi, the agent](/agent).

## Earth

A globe of your taste. Fly to a city and columns rise wherever your taste lives, square by square, coloured by how strong it is. Switch the lens to a friend's taste or a circle's, or filter to music or film. Tap a square to read its **scene**: the artists, brands, places and words of the people around that spot, with a button to plan a night there. The **radar** shows the people whose taste matches yours most, among those who turned it on (Settings), and the public figures Qloo predicts for you.

## You

- **Your taste**: your loves, your words, and your picks across every kind of thing.
- **Graph**: your taste as a web you can play with. Drag a love's weight and watch everything re-rank, grow any node to see what it leads to, overlay a friend to see what you share, and slide back through your history.
- **Your feeds**: one platform at a time (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Each has a **feed score** (screenshot your For You page and see how much of it is you), today's accounts to follow, the topics to teach it, and a way to bring in what you follow. Connect X or Instagram and switch on **Do it for me**: Najimi follows that day's picks for you each morning, slowly.
- **Passport**: a year of stamps (loves, nights, trips, games), how your taste moved month by month, and your **Wrapped**, five story slides you can send straight to Instagram.
- **Business**: plans for the places you run. See below.
- **Settings**: who sees your taste, notifications, today's allowances, your share links, and *Delete all my data*.

## Plans and booking

Ask "9pm in Lekki, we want to go out" or "Lisbon for three days". Najimi plans it from Qloo: stops in order with the real route between them, how well each stop fits each person, and shows on that night. Every plan has its own page with a map and *Add to calendar*.

- **Hotels** book for real through Nuitee: you pay in Nuitee's own payment sheet, and you can cancel from the card.
- **Tables**: Najimi can phone the venue for you. You approve the call first; it says it's an AI and that it's recorded, and only calls between 8am and 9pm. The result comes back into your chat.
- The next day, Najimi asks how it was. Your answer becomes part of your taste.

## Business mode

Tell Najimi about your business (or use **Your business** → **Get my plan**): its name, what it is, where it is, and your site if you have one. It reads your site, finds you in Qloo (or places like you), and builds a plan:

- who your people are and what else they love, by kind;
- where they cluster in your city, in 3D;
- your street's own scene, and what's only yours versus only the street's;
- places rising nearby;
- ideas (a night, a menu item, an artist to book, a brand to team up with), each showing the Qloo results it stands on, and posts to try.

Share the plan with a link. Every Monday it's rebuilt, and you hear what changed.
