Najimi would not work without [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com). Someone who loves *Insecure* and *Americanah* tends to love Jorja Smith, a certain kind of restaurant, a certain kind of room: Qloo knows those links across music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations, games and people. Every pick, word, map, match and plan stop in Najimi is a Qloo answer, and so are the hidden answers in *Guess my wavelength* and *Blind Taste*.

## The endpoints

| Endpoint | What Najimi asks it |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /search` | Turn a name into a Qloo id: the loves you pick, cities, a business near its address, imports, the agent's lookups. |
| `GET /entities` | One thing's full record (the [sheet](/docs/guide/things) for anything you tap); imports matched exactly by Goodreads, MusicBrainz, X or Instagram id; poll options' artwork. |
| `POST /v2/insights` | The workhorse. Your loves go in as weighted `{ id, weight }` signals with **explainability** on. See the uses below. |
| `GET /v2/tags` | Find tags by meaning: "candlelit", "jazz bar", "rainy day", a business's kind. |
| `GET /v2/analysis/compare` | What two tastes share and what's only each one's: your match with anyone, a business against its street, the agent's comparisons. |
| `GET /v2/audiences` | Audiences the agent can lean a taste with. |

### The many shapes of `/v2/insights`

| Ask | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `filter.type` = an entity kind, weighted signal | Your picks in ten kinds, every page of the feed, more like this, the graph's growth, games, tune-up accounts |
| `filter.type` = `urn:tag` | Your taste's words: aesthetics, decor, ambience, style, cuisines, dishes |
| `filter.type` = `urn:heatmap` | Where a taste lives in a city, square by square: Earth, trip areas, a business's people |
| `filter.type` = `urn:demographics` | Who a taste leans toward by age and gender: business plans, the agent |
| `signal.location`, no person | A spot's own scene within 2.5 km: Earth's scene sheet, a business's street |
| `filter.results.entities` | How well a shortlist fits someone: *Guess my wavelength* and *Blind Taste* answers, each friend's fit in a group, the feed score |
| `audience_growth` | Places rising among a business's fans |

## The agent gets the whole surface

Najimi isn't handed a "recommend" button. Its five Qloo tools mirror the API itself, and it composes them for each ask:

| Tool | Endpoint | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `qlooSearch` | `/search` | names → ids |
| `qlooTags` | `/v2/tags` | meanings → tags |
| `qlooInsights` | `/v2/insights` | picks, words, heatmaps, demographics, with Qloo's signals and a wide set of its filters |
| `qlooCompare` | `/v2/analysis/compare` | two tastes side by side |
| `qlooAudiences` | `/v2/audiences` | audiences to lean with |

See [Every tool](/docs/agent/tools#qloo) for every filter and how it composes them ("hidden-gem music" is artists with a low popularity ceiling; "is Burna Boy for me?" scores exactly his id against your taste).

## Fast, cached and fair

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="One pipe">
Every call goes through one client: at most four at a time per process, three tries on `429`, `500`, `502`, `503` and `504` with jittered backoff (honouring `Retry-After`), a 20-second timeout. Identical requests already in flight share one call.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Cached by meaning">
Answers are kept in Postgres under a key made of the path, the parameters and the body: 30 days for search, entities, tags and audiences; 7 days for insights and compare. A reload or a retry costs Qloo nothing. Only successful answers are kept.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Logged, every time">
Every request (cached or not) is logged with who it was for, the chat and message, a plain-words purpose, the time and the result count. That log is what the Qloo chips and *Why this?* in the app open. Qloo's answers stay on the server; the public repository holds none of its data.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
