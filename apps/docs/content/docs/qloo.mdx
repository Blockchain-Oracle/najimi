Najimi would not work without [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com). Qloo's taste graph knows how things people love connect across domains: someone who loves *Insecure* and *Americanah* tends to love Jorja Smith, a certain kind of restaurant, a certain kind of room. Najimi asks Qloo, then turns the answers into things you can use.

- **Weighted signals.** Each love goes in as `{ id, weight }`, so a love at 9 pulls harder than one at 3.
- **Explainability.** Qloo says which love drove each result. That is the **because** chip on every pick, and the "what your taste connects" line ("Insecure led to Jorja Smith").
- **Words.** Tag insights over places and media give the vocabulary your taste is made of. Those words shape everything Najimi makes: the restyle's prompt, the lighting plan's mood, the recipe's cuisine.

## The agent's Qloo tools

The chat's model gets Qloo's whole surface as tools, not a narrow "recommend" button, and composes them per ask.

| Tool | Qloo endpoint | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `qlooSearch` | `/search` | Turn a name ("Fela", "Kyoto") into a Qloo id to use as a signal, filter or comparison. |
| `qlooTags` | `/v2/tags` | Find tags by meaning: moods ("rainy day"), genres, cuisines, decor, dietary options, place kinds. |
| `qlooInsights` | `/v2/insights` | Anything the graph answers: picks of any type, a taste's words, a **heatmap** of a city, the **demographic** lean. Every documented signal and filter. |
| `qlooCompare` | `/v2/analysis/compare` | Two tastes side by side: you and Mum, or *Fleabag* vs *Insecure*. |
| `qlooAudiences` | `/v2/audiences` | Audiences (life stage, hobbies, communities) to lean a taste with. Never political ones. |

`qlooInsights` takes the person's loves as the signal by default, and the model adds to it freely:

| Ask | How the model composes it |
| --- | --- |
| "70s films I'd love" | `ask: movie`, `filter.releaseYear {1970–1979}` |
| "Hidden gems" | `filter.popularity { max: 0.3 }` |
| "Where would I hang out in Paris?" | `ask: heatmap`, `filter.city: Paris` → a map with named areas |
| "Is Kendrick a fit for me?" | `filter.onlyIds: [his id]` → a score |
| "Dinner for Mum" | `signal.age: 55_and_older`, `filter.tags: [restaurant]`, her city |
| "A trip around my taste" | `ask: destination`, `diversifyBy: city` |
| "Music for this room" | `qlooTags "candlelit"` → `signal.tags` |

Each answer becomes a card that renders whatever came back: picks of any type, a tag cloud, a map, an age and gender chart, a comparison.

## Seeing every call

Every Qloo request is logged with its path, parameters, status, time, cache hit and result count.

- On every Qloo card, the **Qloo** chip opens the exact request behind it.
- On a taste page, **Built on Qloo** lists every call the reveal made, in order.
- **Compare without taste** answers the same ask with no Qloo at all, beside Najimi's answer.

Answers are cached by a stable key (path, parameters and body), and identical requests in flight share one call, so a page reload or a retry costs Qloo nothing.
