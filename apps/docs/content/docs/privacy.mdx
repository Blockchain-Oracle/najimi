## What's kept

- **Your account:** a guest account, or your email or Google sign-in.
- **People and tastes:** names, cities, loves and weights, taste words and picks, and each person's About (diet, allergies, texture, medications) if you add it.
- **Chats:** your messages and Najimi's answers.
- **Photos and creations:** in private storage. Nobody can open your photos but you.
- **Usage:** today's count of messages, images and videos, for the allowance.

## What leaves Najimi

To answer, Najimi sends what's needed and nothing more:

| Service | What it gets |
| --- | --- |
| Qloo | Qloo ids of your loves and their weights, tags, a city when you ask about places. Never your name. |
| OpenAI | The conversation and context for the reply. |
| fal | Your photo and the prompt when you ask for a restyle; the prompt for a new picture. |
| OpenFreeMap | Your browser loads its map tiles when you pick your city at sign-up. |
| ElevenLabs | The recording, when you dictate. |
| openFDA | A medication name, to read its label. |

## Sharing

Nothing is public until you share it, and a share shows only what it says:

- **A creation:** the result. Never your own photo.
- **A chat:** the words and cards. Your photos are removed before the page is built.
- **A taste:** loves, words, connections and picks. Never the city, age or About.

**Settings → Your share links** lists every live link. **Stop sharing** ends one at once.

## Deleting

**Settings → Delete all my data** removes your account and everything in it: people, tastes, chats, photos, creations, workflows and share links. Rows go first, in one transaction, then the stored files. It can't be undone.

Guest accounts nobody has used for 30 days are deleted the same way, every night.
