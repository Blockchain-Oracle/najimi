title: Privacy
description: What Najimi keeps, what it shares, and how to delete all of it.

## What's kept

- **Your account:** a guest account, or an email sign-in, and your username.
- **Your taste:** your loves and their weights, your taste words and picks, what you loved or skipped in the feed, your city if you give one.
- **Chats:** your messages, Najimi's answers, and the circles you're in.
- **Plans, bookings and games:** what you planned, booked and played (so Najimi can follow up and stamp your Passport).
- **Connected accounts:** if you connect X or Instagram, your session is **sealed** (AES-256-GCM) the moment it arrives, opened only on the server to act for you, never shown again, and deleted when you disconnect.
- **Businesses:** what you told Najimi about them and the plans it built.
- **Usage:** today's counts for the allowance, and what the models cost for you.

## What leaves Najimi

Only what's needed for the thing you asked:

| Service | What it gets |
| --- | --- |
| Qloo | Qloo ids of your loves and their weights, tags, a city or a point on the map. Never your name. |
| OpenAI | The conversation and context for a reply; a feed screenshot you score (it's read, never kept). |
| Nuitee | Your name, email and phone when you book a room. You pay in Nuitee's own sheet; Najimi never sees your card. |
| ElevenLabs and Twilio | The venue's number, your first name and the booking details, for a call you approved. |
| X and Instagram | Your own session, only to read what you follow and act when you said so. |
| Mapbox, Ticketmaster, Apple, TMDB, Wikidata, OpenStreetMap | Places, times and names to look up. Never who you are. |
| Your push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla) | The notification itself, sent to the phone you turned push on for. |

## Who sees your taste

**Settings → Who sees your taste**: everyone, only friends (people who follow each other), or only you. Private tastes don't appear in search, twins, the radar or the map.

**Block** someone from their profile and you disappear from each other: search, twins, the radar, the map and profiles. Follows stop, and neither of you can message the other. **Report** keeps a note for review and blocks them too.

## Sharing

Nothing is public until you share it, and a share shows only what it says: a taste card (never your city), a game, a plan, a business plan, your Wrapped. **Settings → Your share links** lists every live link; **Stop sharing** ends one at once.

## Deleting

**Settings → Delete all my data** removes your account and everything in it: your taste, chats, plans, games, connected sessions, businesses, push subscriptions, files and share links. It can't be undone.

Guest accounts nobody has used for 30 days are deleted the same way, every night.
