A **turn** is one answer from Najimi. It runs in the background worker, never inside your page, and streams to everyone in the room as it goes.

## Step by step

<Steps>
<Step>
**Your message is saved** and announced on the room's live channel. If it calls Najimi (always in your own chat; `@najimi` or "Najimi…" in a circle or DM), one turn of your daily allowance is used and a turn is queued for that room.
</Step>
<Step>
**The worker picks it up.** Each room runs one turn at a time, so answers never cross. Up to eight rooms are answered at once.
</Step>
<Step>
**Najimi reads the room**: your taste and, in a circle, everyone's; the screen you were on; your plans, bookings and connected accounts; the room's polls; photos shared here; the whole conversation.
</Step>
<Step>
**It works**: tool calls, several at once when they're independent, up to ten steps. The growing answer is saved and sent to the room about every 150 milliseconds.
</Step>
<Step>
**Cards that wait.** A call, a post, a picture in *Ask me first*, or a question card pauses the turn. When it's answered (an approval only by the person who asked), a new turn picks up exactly where it stopped. Approvals are signed, so nobody can forge a yes.
</Step>
<Step>
**Done.** The answer is final, the run folds into one line (*5 steps · 5 Qloo calls*), and a new chat gets a title in your language.
</Step>
</Steps>

Because the turn lives in the worker, it finishes even if you close the app; anyone who opens the room later sees it.

## Stop and try again

- **Stop** ends a turn within a second and keeps what was already said.
- **Try again** removes the last answer and runs it fresh. It doesn't use a turn of your allowance.
- If the AI provider fails, the answer says so, with **Try again**.

## What it knows each turn

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | name, city, every love with its weight, your taste's words |
| **The room** | in a circle or DM: each member's name, city and top five loves |
| **Where you are** | the screen you opened it from and the thing on it (a feed card, a pick, a square on Earth, a graph node, a plan, a business plan) |
| **Your life in Najimi** | plans and bookings from the last 30 days, and how they went; your connected accounts |
| **The room's latest** | the last three polls with their votes; photos shared; what Najimi made here |
| **When** | today's date in your time zone; it replies in your language |

## Models and cost

| Work | Model |
| --- | --- |
| Every answer in every room, reading a feed screenshot, writing a business plan | `gpt-6.1-sol` (OpenAI) |
| Chat titles, reading a business's website, picking culture names out of posts | `gpt-6-luna` (OpenAI), a small, cheap model |
| Web search | OpenAI's hosted search, with your city as a rough location |

Every model call is logged with its tokens, time and cost in dollars, and **Settings** shows what Najimi's thinking cost you this month.
