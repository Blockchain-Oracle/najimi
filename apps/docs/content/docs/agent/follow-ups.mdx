Najimi doesn't stop at the answer. A background worker keeps going for you, and everything it finds lands where you'll see it: in the chat it came from, in your inbox, and on your phone.

| What | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Call results** | a few minutes after a call | The answer from the venue lands in the chat as a booking card. If ElevenLabs' report doesn't arrive, Najimi checks every three minutes for half an hour, then tells you it couldn't get a clear answer and nothing is booked. |
| **Hotel confirmations** | after you pay | If the hotel hasn't confirmed, Najimi asks Nuitee every ten minutes for a day and tells you either way. |
| **How was it?** | 3 hours after a table, the morning after a stay, 12 hours after a night out starts, the day after a one- or two-day trip ends | A card in the chat: *Loved it*, *It was fine*, *Not for me*, *We didn't go*. If you went, it becomes a Passport stamp; if you loved a place you booked, Najimi can add it to your taste. |
| **Today's drop** | 9am in every active circle | A *Guess my wavelength* about one member, taking turns, and a notification for everyone. |
| **Business, every Monday** | Monday morning | Every plan used in the last 45 days is rebuilt with fresh Qloo data, and you hear what's rising among your people. |
| **A business plan you asked for in chat** | about a minute later | *"Mama's Kitchen's plan is ready… Its people cluster most in A and B. I'd start with this…"*, posted back into the chat you asked in. |
| **Do it for me** | 9am your time | On X and Instagram, if you switched it on: follows that day's tune-up accounts with your session, slowly, and tells you who. Stops for a day at the first push-back. |
| **Your tune-up** | 8am your time | *"Your five-minute feed tune-up is ready"*, if you've used it this week. |

## Notifications

Everything above, and your follows, invites, circle messages and games, reaches your phone as a notification once you've turned them on (on iPhone, with Najimi on your Home Screen). Booking and business news arrives from **Najimi**. Choose which groups you get in [Settings](/docs/guide/settings#notifications).
