## Where it lives

- **Your own chat** (the orb in the middle of the tab bar): it answers every message.
- **Circles and DMs**: it answers when someone writes `@najimi` or starts with "Najimi…", and speaks for the whole room, by name.
- **Over any screen**: tap the orb and it opens as a sheet that already knows what you're looking at: the card on your feed, a square on Earth, a node in your graph, the plan you're reading, a business plan. "More like this" means *that*.

## What it knows

Your loves and their weights, your taste's words, and in a room every member's top loves and city. The screen you opened it from, your plans and bookings from the last month (and how they went), the room's latest polls, your connected accounts, the photos shared in the chat, and today's date in your time zone. It replies in your language.

## The rules it follows

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Qloo or nothing">
Every recommendation comes from a Qloo result in this chat. It never names a pick from memory. If Qloo has nothing, it says so and tries another way.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It asks before anything real">
A phone call and a post in your name always wait for your yes, with exactly what will happen shown first. In *Ask me first* mode, so does a picture. In a circle, only the person who asked can say yes. Hotels are only ever paid by you, in Nuitee's sheet. If you say no, it doesn't ask again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It never claims what isn't done">
Nothing is "booked" until the booking card says confirmed, and nothing is "posted" until the post card says so.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It asks only when it matters">
At most one card with up to three questions, and only when the answer would really change the result. Anyone in the room can answer it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Brief and warm">
One or two sentences that connect the results to what you love ("because you love Fleabag…"). The cards carry the detail; it never repeats them as a list.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It follows up">
Calls report back into the chat. After a night, a table or a stay, it asks how it was; tell it you loved a place you booked and it can add it to your taste.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
