Most of what Najimi does is reading Qloo. Four things reach outside the app. Calls and posts always show you what will happen first, and so do pictures in *Ask me first*. Calls, room holds and pictures have a daily allowance. All are logged.

| Action | Who says yes | Allowance a day (guest / signed in) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A phone call to a venue | you, on the call card, every time | none / 3 |
| A post on X | you, on the post card, every time | — |
| Holding a hotel room | you, by tapping **Book**; you pay in Nuitee's sheet | 2 / 6 |
| A picture | you, on a card, in *Ask me first* mode | 10 / 30 |

In a circle or DM, **only the person who asked** can say yes; the server refuses anyone else. If you say no, Najimi doesn't ask again.

## Phone calls

Ask "book us a table at NOK for four at 8 on Saturday". Najimi needs the venue, the time and how many; it finds the phone number on the venue's website if you don't give one.

<Steps>
<Step>

### You see the call first

A call card shows how the call will open (on the call itself, Najimi gives your name):

> Hello, is this NOK by Alara? I'm Najimi, an AI assistant calling for you. This call is recorded. I'd like to book a table for 4 on Saturday 24 October at 20:00.

and the rules: *a real phone call, between 8am and 9pm; Najimi never pays or agrees to a deposit; you'll see the answer here.* **Call** or **Not now**.

</Step>
<Step>

### It calls

An ElevenLabs voice agent dials from a Twilio number and opens by saying it's an AI calling on your behalf and that the call is recorded, then asks for your table. It speaks in short sentences, confirms the date, time, party size and your name once, asks for the nearest time without accepting it if yours isn't possible, and never pays, shares card details or agrees to a deposit. It presses keys to get through a phone menu, hangs up without a message if it reaches voicemail, and ends the call itself once the booking is settled or as soon as they say goodbye (or after 20 seconds of silence). The card says *Calling…*, then *Called* with the outcome.

</Step>
<Step>

### The answer comes back

A few minutes later, a booking card in the chat: **booked** (with the name it's under, **Add to calendar**), or the time they offered instead, or *the line was busy* or *I reached their voicemail*, with *want me to try again later?* You also get a notification. Results arrive by a signed webhook from ElevenLabs, and Najimi checks every few minutes in case the webhook doesn't come.

</Step>
<Step>

### Afterwards

Three hours after your table, Najimi asks how it was.

</Step>
</Steps>

Calls only go out between 8am and 9pm **where the venue is** (read from its number's country; your own time zone when the country spans several). A venue that asks not to be called by an AI again is never called again: Najimi gives you its number instead. Guests can't make calls; sign in first.

## Hotels

<Callout title="On najimi.xyz" type="info">
Hotels switch on when Nuitee's live key is added to the server. Until then Najimi says plainly that hotel booking isn't on yet, while nights, routes and shows work as described.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>
**Ask** for somewhere to stay: where, when, how many nights, how many people. Najimi shows real rooms from Nuitee within a few kilometres, best rated first, with the price for the whole stay and whether it's refundable.
</Step>
<Step>
**Book** holds that price with Nuitee. Nothing is charged.
</Step>
<Step>
**Pay** on the payment page: your name, email and phone, then **Nuitee's own payment sheet**. Nuitee is the merchant of record; Najimi never sees your card.
</Step>
<Step>
**Confirmed** lands in the chat as a booking card: the confirmation, **Add to calendar**, and **Cancel** while it's free to. If the hotel is slow, Najimi checks every ten minutes for a day and tells you either way.
</Step>
</Steps>

The morning after you check out, it asks how it was.

## Posts on X

Ask Najimi to post something ("tell my followers about tonight"). It drafts the post in your voice and shows it as a preview with a character count. **Post it** sends it from your connected X; the card turns into the posted post with its link. Without a connected X, you get a one-tap link that opens X with the words filled in. If X refuses, you keep the words and the link.

Connect X on [Your feeds](/docs/guide/feeds).

## Pictures

Share a photo of your room and ask "show me this in my taste". Najimi writes the picture from your taste's words (only words that really are in your Qloo taste) and keeps your room exactly as it is: the furniture, the layout, the camera angle. It can also make a new picture from your words. It never offers one unprompted.

In *Ask me first* mode a card shows what it will make and what it uses of today's allowance. The picture arrives with the before and after, download, share and a variation. Pictures are made by FLUX 2 on fal, or OpenAI's image models. If a picture fails, its allowance comes back.

| | Pictures | Calls and posts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask me first** (default) | a card first | always a card first |
| **Just do it** | made straight away | always a card first |
| **Only suggest** | never made; described instead | always a card first |
