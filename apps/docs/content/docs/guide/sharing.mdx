Every share is a link with its own preview card (drawn by Najimi, or a map for a plan; never from your own photos). Anyone can open it without an account.

| What | Where you share it from | What the link shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your taste card** | **Share** on your profile | Your name, face, loves, words and picks. Never your city or bio. |
| **A chat** | **Share this chat** under an answer | The conversation, read-only, with your own photos taken out. |
| **A picture Najimi made** | the picture's card | The picture. Never your own photo. |
| **A plan** | every plan has one | The route, the stops, calendar links. |
| **A game** | the challenge link | The round, playable by anyone. |
| **A business plan** | **Share** on the plan | The whole plan, read-only. |
| **A circle invite** | the circle's Invite card | A way in: newcomers pick a few loves and join. |
| **Your Wrapped** | Passport | The five slides, playing like a story. |

## Stop sharing

**Settings → Your share links** lists every live link you've made, newest first, each with **Stop sharing**.

- A taste card, a chat, a picture, a business plan or a Wrapped stops working at once.
- A plan or a game stays open only to you and the people in its chat.
- A circle invite gets a new link; the old one dies.

Link previews are cached briefly, so a stopped link's preview fades within minutes. Shared pages are never indexed by search engines.
