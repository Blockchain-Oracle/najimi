Your social feeds learn from what you follow. **Your feeds** (on your profile) uses your taste to steer them back toward you, one platform at a time: **X**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, **YouTube**.

## Feed score

Screenshot your *For You*, *Explore* or *Home* page and upload it. Najimi reads the names, handles and topics on screen, finds each one in Qloo, and scores it against your taste: the ring shows **how much of this feed is you**. The screenshot is read once and never stored; your scores are kept so you can see them move.

## Today's tune-up

Every day, a short list of real accounts (people, artists, brands) that Qloo matched to what you love, each with its reason. **Follow** opens the profile (or a search for it on TikTok and YouTube) and ticks it off. If you've used it this week, a morning notification says when the next one is ready.

## Teach the algorithm

Your taste as topics, ready to copy into Instagram's *Your Algorithm* or TikTok's *Manage topics*.

## Connect, and "Do it for me"

On **X** and **Instagram** you can connect your own account by pasting your session from the browser. It's sealed (AES-256-GCM) the moment it arrives, only opened on the server to act for you, and deleted when you disconnect.

Then switch on **Do it for me**: each morning at 9am your time, Najimi follows that day's tune-up accounts for you, **slowly** (a random pause between each, at most 25 a day on X and 15 on Instagram). It tells you as it goes and when it's done, and at the first sign the platform objects it stops for a day and says so.

TikTok and YouTube can't be connected; use their data exports below.

## Bring what you already follow

- **X**: your account read by handle.
- **Exports**, opened right in your browser (only a short list leaves it): Instagram (following, liked, saved), TikTok (`user_data.json`), Spotify (*YourLibrary.json*), YouTube Takeout (subscriptions).
- **By name**: Letterboxd, Goodreads, Last.fm, or a list of handles.

Everything is matched to Qloo, and you choose what joins your taste.
