Your social feeds learn from what you follow. **Your feeds** (on your profile) uses your taste to steer them back toward you, one platform at a time: **X**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, **YouTube**.

## Feed score

Screenshot your *For You*, *Explore* or *Home* page and upload it. Najimi reads the names, handles and topics on screen, finds each one in Qloo, and scores it against your taste: the ring shows **how much of this feed is you**. The screenshot is read once and never stored; your scores are kept so you can see them move.

## Today's tune-up

Every day, a short list of real accounts (people, artists, brands) that Qloo matched to what you love, each with its reason. **Follow** opens the profile (or a search for it on TikTok and YouTube) and ticks it off. If you've used it this week, a morning notification says when the next one is ready.

## Teach the algorithm

Your taste as topics, ready to copy into Instagram's *Your Algorithm* or TikTok's *Manage topics*.

## Connect, and "Do it for me"

X and Instagram have no official way for an app to follow or post for a person, so on those two Najimi acts through **your own signed-in session**. The sheet says that first, shows the risk (platforms can limit accounts that automate) before you paste anything, and shows where the session cookie lives in your browser. The session is sealed (AES-256-GCM) the moment it arrives, only opened on the server to act for you, and deleted when you disconnect.

Then switch on the agents you want, on this page or all together under **Your agents** (Settings → Your agents):

| Agent | What it does | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Follow for me** (*Do it for me*) | Follows that day's tune-up accounts, **slowly** (a random pause between each, at most 25 a day on X and 15 on Instagram) | 9am your time |
| **Like for me** | Likes the newest post of accounts in your taste (today's tune-up and the ones Najimi followed for you), at most 40 a day on X and 30 on Instagram | each morning |
| **Post for me** | Writes one post from your week in taste (your loves and your taste's words; never plans, places or people). **Ask me first** keeps it as a draft for your yes, which you can edit; **Just do it** posts it on X itself. On Instagram it writes the words and you add the picture | Sunday evening, or **Write one now** |

Each one tells you as it goes, and **Your agents** shows what each did lately. **Pause a day** stops them all on that account; at the first sign a platform objects, Najimi pauses it for a day itself and says so.

**YouTube** connects with Google instead, read-only: **Connect YouTube** asks Google for permission to read your subscriptions and liked videos, and nothing else (it never likes, subscribes or posts). It appears once Google sign-in is switched on for the server. TikTok can't be connected to act for you.

## Bring what you already follow

**Your data, in two taps.** Every platform lets you download what you follow and love. In each platform's sheet:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Ask for your file.** One tap opens the platform's own download page, with a line on what to tick. Najimi remembers you asked, and when the file is usually ready it reminds you (a notification that opens the same sheet).
</Step>
<Step>
**Choose the file** when it arrives. It's opened in your browser; only a short list leaves it (handles, artist and channel names, and a few dozen titles or posts to read for the names in them).
</Step>
</Steps>

| Platform | Usually ready | What Najimi reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **X** | about a day | What you liked (the culture named in it). X's file lists who you follow only by number, so connect X or read your handle for those. Bookmarks aren't in X's file. |
| **Instagram** | a few hours | Who you follow, and whose posts you liked and saved. |
| **TikTok** | a few days | Who you follow, and your favourite sounds. Your likes and watch history are video links with no creator, so they're left out. |
| **YouTube** (Google Takeout) | within the hour | The artists you play most on YouTube Music, the channels you subscribe to, and what you watched. |
| **Spotify** | up to five days | The artists and shows in your library. |

Also: **X** read by handle (who an account shares and mentions), and **by name** on Letterboxd, Goodreads, Last.fm, or a list of handles.

Everything is matched to Qloo, and you choose what joins your taste.
