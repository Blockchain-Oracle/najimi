Stories live where people already are. The **tray** sits at the top of People: your story first (with **+**), then everyone you follow who has something up. A **ring** on someone's face (on their profile, in your chats list, in a circle) means they have a story; it glows in the aura while there's one you haven't seen, and goes quiet once you have.

## Post one

Tap **Your story** (or **+**) and pick what to share:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo or video**, or the **Camera** | A photo can carry **a song** that plays with it. Videos are trimmed to a minute and made phone-ready. |
| **A song** | One from your taste, played from Apple's preview. |
| **A pick** | Something from your taste, with its artwork. |
| **A plan** | One of your nights or trips that hasn't happened yet. |
| **Wrapped** | One of your Wrapped slides (also from **Post to your Najimi story** on the [Passport](/docs/guide/passport)). |

Add a caption if you like, then **Share to your story**. It stays up for **24 hours**, and you can delete it any time from **More**.

## Watching

Tap a ring and the stories play full screen: bars across the top, tap right or left to move, hold to pause, swipe down to close. When one person's stories end, the next person's start. A song's name sits under the poster's name, and a sticker opens the thing or the plan it's about. Sound starts on, or a button turns it on where the browser asks for a tap first.

## Replies and reactions

Reply, or react with ❤️ 🔥 😂 😮 😢 👏: both land in your DM with them, quoting the story, and they get a notification. On your own stories you see **who saw them**, with their reactions.

## Who sees them

The same people who can see your taste (**Everyone**, **Friends** or **Only me**, in **Settings → Who sees your taste**). Blocks hold both ways. After a day, a story and its files are deleted.
