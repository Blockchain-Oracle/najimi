Tap **Earth**. The globe flies to your city and raises a column on every small square of the map where your taste lives: taller and warmer where it's stronger. The strongest areas get their names. It's Qloo's **heatmap** of your taste, the same one behind your profile's *Your people* line.

## Change what you're looking at

- **Lens**: your taste, a friend's, or a circle's (everyone's loves blended so each person counts the same). You can only use a lens on a taste you're allowed to see.
- **Kind**: everything, or just music, places, TV, film, brands, books, podcasts or games.
- **City**: search any city; **Back to the city** returns you.
- **Your people on the globe**: public people with a city, placed near their city (never at an exact spot).

## A spot's scene

Tap a column or a named area and its **scene** opens: the taste of the people around that spot, read from Qloo with nothing but the location as the signal.

- **How much it's you**, as a percentage.
- **Feels like**: its words. **Sounds like**: its artists. **Where people go**: its places. **What they wear and use**: its brands.
- **Your people near here**, with how far they are.
- Three buttons that hand the spot to Najimi: **Plan a night** there, **Ask Najimi** about it, or **Plan a business here**.

## The radar

The radar button puts you in the middle of rings: the people on Najimi whose taste is closest to yours on the inner rings, and public figures Qloo predicts for you on the outer one. Only people who switched **Show me on the radar** on in Settings appear; it isn't limited to people nearby.
