Open **Business** from your profile and tap **New plan**, or just tell Najimi "I run a café in Yaba". It asks for what it needs in one card: what the place is, its name, the city and street or area, and if you like its website, who you think comes, and what you're deciding.

## How the plan is built

Every step shows live, on the card in the chat and on the plan's page.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Your site.** If you have one, Najimi reads what you offer and how you describe yourself.
</Step>
<Step>
**Find you in Qloo.** The business itself near its address, or, if Qloo doesn't have it yet, up to five places like it nearby.
</Step>
<Step>
**Your fans.** What your people love across music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands and places, the words of their taste, and who they lean toward by age and gender.
</Step>
<Step>
**Where they are.** Qloo's heatmap of your city for your fans, with the strongest five areas named, drawn in 3D.
</Step>
<Step>
**Your street.** The taste of the street around you, then a Qloo comparison: what's only your fans', what you share, and what's only the street's.
</Step>
<Step>
**Rising.** Nearby places whose audience is growing among your fans.
</Step>
<Step>
**The plan.** Najimi writes up to six ideas (a menu item, music, a night, stock, a partner, the space, the service), partners to team up with, three posts, and the areas to reach. **Every idea shows the Qloo results it stands on**; anything that can't point to real evidence is dropped.
</Step>
</Steps>

## On the plan's page

A map with where your people cluster, who they lean toward and what's rising nearby, Najimi's read, the ideas, what your fans love by kind, your street, partners, and posts (copy, or post on X). **Share** makes a public link; **Build it again** runs it again; **Ask Najimi** talks it through. Share a photo of your space and Najimi can restyle it in your taste's words.

## Every Monday

Each plan you've used in the last 45 days is rebuilt every Monday, and you hear what moved: *"This week around Mama's Kitchen: A, B are rising among your people."* You can build six plans a day.
