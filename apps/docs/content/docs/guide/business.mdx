Open **Business** from your profile and tap **New plan**, or just tell Najimi "I run a café in Yaba". It asks for what it needs in one card: what the place is, its name, the city and street or area, and if you like its website, who you think comes, and what you're deciding.

## How the plan is built

Every step shows live, on the card in the chat and on the plan's page.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Your site.** If you have one, Najimi reads what you offer and how you describe yourself.
</Step>
<Step>
**Find you in Qloo.** The business itself near its address, or, if Qloo doesn't have it yet, up to five places like it nearby.
</Step>
<Step>
**Your fans.** What your people love across music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands and places, the words of their taste, and who they lean toward by age and gender.
</Step>
<Step>
**Where they are.** Qloo's heatmap of your city for your fans, with the strongest five areas named, drawn in 3D.
</Step>
<Step>
**Your street.** The taste of the street around you, then a Qloo comparison: what's only your fans', what you share, and what's only the street's.
</Step>
<Step>
**Rising.** Nearby places whose audience is growing among your fans.
</Step>
<Step>
**The plan.** Najimi writes one sentence on who your people are and **three moves for the week** (a night, music, a partner, the menu, stock, the space, the service), each in a few words with one line of why. **Every move shows the Qloo results it stands on**; anything that can't point to real evidence is dropped. It also writes posts, partners to team up with and the areas to reach.
</Step>
</Steps>

## On the plan's page

**Your people** comes first: the sentence, the things they love as faces (tap one to open it), and three chips for who they lean toward, where they cluster and what's rising nearby.

Then **This week**: the three moves. Tap one for its why, what it stands on, and its one next step:

| Next step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Post it** | The post is written; **Post** opens X with it, or share it anywhere, or copy it |
| **Invite** | A first message to the partner is written; one tap copies it and opens their Instagram (or X, TikTok, their site) |
| **Plan it** | Najimi plans it in your chat: the night, the day, who it's for |
| **Play the set** | The artists it stands on play through the app's one player |

<Shot src="/shots/wide/business-recommendation.webp" alt="A move opened: invite April By Kunbi, the brand it stands on, and the message ready to send" caption="A move opened: why, what it stands on, the message to send, and one tap to send it." />

The rest sits behind tabs: **People** (what they love by kind, their words, and your people against the street), **Places** (the city in 3D, where they cluster, your place or places like it, partners, what's rising and what's around you; each opens with its map, call and socials), and **Posts**. **Share** makes a public link; **Build it again** runs it again; **Ask Najimi** talks it through. Share a photo of your space in the chat and Najimi can restyle it in your taste's words.

## Every Monday

Each plan you've used in the last 45 days is rebuilt every Monday, and you hear what moved: *"This week around Mama's Kitchen: A, B are rising among your people."* You can build six plans a day.
