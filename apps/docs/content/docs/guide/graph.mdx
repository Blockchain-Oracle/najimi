Open **Graph** from your profile. You're in the middle, your loves around you, and Qloo's picks beyond them. Each tie is as strong as Qloo's explainability says: how much that love led to that pick.

## What you can do

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="SlidersHorizontal" />} title="What if">
    Tap a love and turn its dial: every pick re-ranks at once from the explainability shares. **Make it real** asks Qloo again with the new weights; **Undo** puts it back.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="Sprout" />} title="Grow from here">
    Tap any node to ask Qloo what it leads to: more of its own kind and one related kind (an artist grows places, a book grows films).
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="Layers" />} title="Lay over">
    Put a friend's taste on top of yours. What you share glows.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="Clock" />} title="Through time">
    Slide from your first love to now and watch the web build.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="Image" />} title="Save the graph">
    As a picture, to share or download.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Icon name="MessageCircle" />} title="Ask Najimi">
    About any node. It already knows which one you're on.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Tapping a node opens its sheet: *why it's here*, how much it counts (for your own loves), **Grow from here** and **Ask Najimi**.
