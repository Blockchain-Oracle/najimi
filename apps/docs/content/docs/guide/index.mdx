**Najimi** (馴染み, *someone you know well*) starts with a few things you love: an artist, a show, a book, a place. [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com)'s taste graph turns them into a taste that reaches every kind of culture: music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations, games and people. Everything in the app is that one taste, seen a different way, and **Najimi**, the agent, uses it to do real things for you and your friends.

It can also **phone a restaurant** for a table, **book a hotel** you pay for in Nuitee's own sheet, **post on X** in your voice, plan a whole night for a group, start a game or a poll, search the web for opening hours, and restyle a photo of your room. Anything that acts in your name waits for your yes. See [The agent](/docs/agent).

## Built on Qloo

<p className="not-prose flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2 text-fd-muted-foreground">Every pick, word, map and match in Najimi is a Qloo result. Every call is logged, and you can open the one behind a pick, a chat card or your taste.</p>

Your loves are sent to Qloo as weighted signals with **explainability** switched on, so every pick knows which of your loves led to it ("because you love *Fleabag*"). The same taste becomes a heatmap of your city, the scene around any spot, a score for each friend in a group plan, and the hidden answer in *Guess my wavelength*. [How Najimi uses Qloo](/docs/qloo) maps every call to its screen.
