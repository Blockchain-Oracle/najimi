Tap **People**. At the top, the [stories](/docs/guide/stories) tray and a search for usernames and names; below them, the arcade of [games](/docs/guide/games), your circles, your friends, and people whose taste is close to yours.

## Following and friends

**Follow** anyone. When two people follow each other, they're **friends**: friends can be added to a new circle and see a taste set to *Friends*. You can message anyone whose taste you can see. Someone's profile shows how many followers they have.

## Your match

Open anyone's profile and Najimi asks Qloo to compare your two tastes. You get **one number**, the loves you both have (*You both love*) and the words you both are (*You're both*).

## People with your taste

- **Taste twins**: the people on Najimi who share the most loves and picks with you, with how many things you have in common.
- **In culture, with your taste**: public figures Qloo predicts for you.
- **The radar** (on Earth): the same twins in rings around you, closest in the middle, and public figures on the outer ring. Only people who switched **Show me on the radar** on in Settings appear.

## Circles

A **circle** is a group chat with Najimi in it.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Make one.** People → **New circle**: a name and any friends you want in it. They get an invite.
</Step>
<Step>
**Invite anyone else.** Tap the circle's title for its members sheet: an **Invite** card with a QR code and a share link. Someone new picks a few loves (as a guest is fine) and lands straight in the circle.
</Step>
<Step>
**Call Najimi.** Start a message with `@najimi` or "Najimi…". It answers for the whole room, by name: who a pick fits, and why.
</Step>
</Steps>

The members sheet also has the circle's **soundtrack** (songs for everyone's blended taste, through the one player), what's **loved in this circle**, the words you all share, and the members.

The **Najimi Lounge** is a public circle that Najimi runs. Join it from People to play against everyone.

## DMs

**Message** on a friend's row or on someone's profile opens a one-to-one chat. Najimi is there too when called: plans and group picks include you both.

## Inbox

The bell on People: follows, invites, people joining your circle, games about your taste, daily drops, bookings and business news. It updates live, and the same things arrive as notifications on your phone (see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings)).

## Block and report

From someone's profile menu:

- **Block**: you disappear from each other's search, twins, radar, Earth and profiles; follows stop, a DM between you closes, and they can't join your circle by link. **Settings → Blocked** lists them, with Unblock.
- **Report** (spam, abuse, a fake account, something else): the report is saved and sent to Najimi's owner, and they're blocked too.
