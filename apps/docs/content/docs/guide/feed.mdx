Tap **Feed**. One card fills the screen; swipe up for the next. Every card comes from your taste, and the ones with a reason show it: **Why this? · Fleabag**.

## What plays

| Card | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Music** | The artist's top songs as 30-second Apple Music previews, one after another, with a scrubber. |
| **Podcasts** | The show's latest episode from its own feed. **Keep playing** lets it carry on while you swipe. |
| **Film and TV** | The poster and its **Trailer**. |
| **Places** | A full-screen photo, the area it's in, and **Directions**. |
| **Books, brands, games** | The cover, the logo or the artwork. |

Tap to play or pause. Double-tap to love.

## The rail

- **Love** adds it to your taste. **Keep** saves it.
- **Ask Najimi** opens a chat about this card.
- **Share** sends the right link for it (the store, IMDb, the map).
- **More** opens [the full sheet](/docs/guide/things) with **More like this** and **Not for me**, which hides it for good (with Undo).

## Why this?

The chip opens **Why it's here**: how well it matches you, a bar for each of your loves showing how much it pulled this card in (Qloo's explainability), and the exact Qloo call it came from.

## Tune it

The sliders button opens the **mixing desk**:

- **Up next**: the next five cards, re-ordering live as you tune.
- **Faders**, one per kind: off, less, normal, more. The feed starts over on the new mix.
- **Knobs**, one per love: turn a love up and what's ahead re-ranks at once, then Qloo refines it in the background.

Or just tell Najimi: "less films", "no podcasts this week", "more like this". It turns the same faders and the feed restarts.

## One player, everywhere

What starts in the feed keeps playing on every screen. A mini player sits above the tab bar; tap it for the full player. It works from the lock screen and headphones too, and in the feed, next and previous move between cards.

## What it learns from

A love joins your taste (if you're at 24 loves, the oldest one the feed added makes room; never one you chose), and your taste is recomputed once things settle. **Not for me** hides a thing for good, and **Keep** saves it. Anything you saw in the last three days won't come back.
