A pick on your profile, **More** on a feed card, a stop in a night plan, a pick on one of Najimi's cards, a thing in a spot's scene on Earth: tap it and the **same sheet** opens, with the same sections in the same order for every kind of thing. A thing only shows the sections it has.

## What's on it, top to bottom

| Section | What it is | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The stage** | A trailer (film, TV), a music video, a record that slides out of the cover and spins while the songs play, or the artwork | everything |
| **Match** | How well it fits you, what it is, the year, the area it's in | everything |
| **Made of your loves** | The faces of the loves that led to it; tap for each one's share | anything Qloo explained |
| **Track list** | Song previews or the latest episode, through the app's one player | artists, podcasts |
| **Getting there** | Directions, **Uber**, **Call**, website and map, and whether it's open now | places |
| **Where to watch** | Stream, rent or buy, with the watch link | film, TV |
| **Where to listen** | Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Podcasts | artists, podcasts |
| **Where to read** | Open Library and Google Books | books |
| **More videos** | Clips and featurettes, or music videos, played in the stage | film, TV, artists |
| **On tour** | The next concerts, in your country first, with tickets | artists |
| **What people say** | TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X searches, plus Letterboxd and IMDb, Google reviews or Goodreads | everything |
| **Follow** | Its own Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Facebook and website | where found |
| **About** | Qloo's description | where Qloo has one |

At the bottom: **Keep**, **More like this** (asks Najimi with this thing in mind), **Ask Najimi**, **Share** (the best link for that kind of thing) and **Not for me**, which hides it from your taste for good.

## Where the data comes from

The card you tapped shows at once; the rest fills in: Qloo's own record of the thing, Apple's song and video previews, TMDB's trailers and where-to-watch, Ticketmaster's tour dates, Wikidata, OpenStreetMap and the thing's own website for its accounts and links. See [Integrations](/docs/build/integrations).

## On a shared page

On a link someone shared (a taste card, a chat, a plan), the sheet opens read-only: you can look, play and share, and nothing is saved to anyone's taste.
