Your **taste** in Najimi is a short list of things you love, each with a weight from 1 to 10. That's all you give it. Qloo's taste graph does the rest: it knows how the things people love connect across every kind of culture, so three loves become a whole picture.

## What Qloo gives back

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Picks of every kind">
For each kind (music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations, video games and people) Qloo returns what fits your taste. Every request asks for **explainability**, so each pick knows which of your loves pulled it in and by how much. That's the **because** you see everywhere: *because you love Fleabag*.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The words of your taste">
Qloo's tags for your taste: its aesthetics, moods, styles, decor, ambience, cuisines and dishes. They shape your profile's summary, the prompt for a restyled photo, and what Najimi says about you.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where it lives">
A Qloo **heatmap** of your city: a score for every small square of the map. Najimi names the strongest areas from Qloo's own places. That's Earth, the "your people" line on your profile, the areas of a trip, and where a business's people cluster.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How well anything fits anyone">
Ask Qloo to score a shortlist against a taste and you get a number per thing. That's how a group plan shows each friend's fit, how *Guess my wavelength* and *Blind Taste* know the right answer, and how your feed score knows how much of your feed is you.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## One taste, everywhere

The same taste flows through every screen, so changing it in one place changes everything:

- **Love** a card in the feed and it joins your taste. **Not for me** hides it for good.
- Drag a love's weight on the **Graph** or in the feed's tune sheet: what's ahead re-ranks at once, then Qloo refines it.
- Tell Najimi "less films" or "more like this": it changes your mix, or adds the thing to your loves.

Your taste is recomputed in the background whenever it changes; a burst of changes is folded into one update.

## Other people's tastes

- **You and a friend:** Qloo compares the two tastes and Najimi turns it into one **match** number, the loves you share, and the words you both are.
- **A circle:** its taste is every member's loves blended so each person counts the same. Earth can show a circle's taste, Najimi plans for it, and it has its own soundtrack.
- **A business:** its taste is the business itself in Qloo, or the places most like it, and its people's.

## Shown, never hidden

Every Qloo request is logged: the endpoint, what was asked, the time, whether it was cached, how many results came back. The **<QlooLogo />** chip on a card in the chat, the **Why this?** chip on a pick and **Built on <QlooLogo />** on your profile each open the exact call behind what you're looking at. Read more in [Explainability](/docs/qloo/explainability).
