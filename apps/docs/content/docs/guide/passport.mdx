Open **Passport** from your profile.

- **This year**: new loves, nights out, trips & stays, and games.
- **Coming up**: plans and bookings that haven't happened yet, each opening its plan or chat.
- **How your taste moved**: month by month, what you added by kind.
- **Stamps** by month: a love, a night, a trip, a stay, a table, a show, a game. Stamps are read from what really happened: plans you said how they went, bookings that were confirmed, games you finished.

## Wrapped

**Make my Wrapped** draws five 9:16 story slides from your year: you, what you love, your taste in words, where your people are, and your passport. **Share to Stories** hands all five pictures to your phone's share sheet, ready to post as a story (on a computer it saves them). **Copy link** copies a link that plays them like a story, for anyone.
