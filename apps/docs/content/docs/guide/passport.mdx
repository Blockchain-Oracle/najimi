Open **Passport** from your profile.

## Your year at a glance

It opens on one number, your **new loves** this year, over a collage of the year's artwork, with what the year leaned into (*A year that leaned into music*) and three rings: **nights out**, **trips & stays** and **games**. A switcher goes back through every year you've had Najimi.

Below it:

- **Coming up**: plans and bookings that haven't happened yet, each opening its plan or chat.
- **How your taste moved**: month by month, what you added by kind.
- **Stamps**, inked where they fell: a love, a night, a trip, a stay, a table, a show, a game. Stamps are read from what really happened: plans you said how they went, bookings that were confirmed, games you finished.

## Wrapped

**Make my Wrapped** draws five 9:16 slides from your year: you, what you love, your taste in words, where your people are, and your passport. **Tap a slide to share it**, one at a time:

- **Share to Instagram Stories**: the picture goes to your phone's share sheet, ready for a story (or it's saved, and you add it in Instagram).
- **Post to your Najimi story**: up for 24 hours, on your ring (see [Stories](/docs/guide/stories)).
- **Save image** and **Copy link**: the link plays all five slides like a story, for anyone.
