Tap **You** in the tab bar. Your face sits on an aura lit in your taste's colours, with your name, username, city and a one-line summary of your taste (tap it for every word). Under it: how many **loves**, **picks** and **kept** things you have.

## What's on it

- **Play my taste** starts your feed. **Share** copies a public link to your taste card. **Edit** changes your name, bio, city and who can see your taste.
- A row of named shortcuts: **Feeds**, **Passport**, **Business**, **Graph** and **Najimi**.
- **Your people**: the parts of your city where your taste is strongest (Qloo's heatmap).
- **What your taste connects**: your loves on one side, you in the middle, and the picks they led to on the other. It opens the [Graph](/docs/guide/graph).
- Three tabs: **Taste** (an artwork grid of your picks, filtered by kind), **Loves** (add, remove and weigh what you love), and **Kept** (things you saved).
- **Built on <QlooLogo />** lists every Qloo call your taste was built from, in order.

## Watching it build

The first time, and whenever your loves change, your taste is computed live in front of you: words arrive, then picks kind by kind, your colours, the areas of your city. If anything fails there's a **Retry**.

## Opening a pick

Tap any pick and it opens [the sheet for that thing](/docs/guide/things): its trailer or songs playing right there, where to watch, listen or read, what people say, and **Made of your loves**, the faces of the loves behind it with how much each counted.

## Someone else's profile

Open anyone from search, a circle or a notification and you see their taste the same way, plus:

- **Your match**: one number from Qloo comparing your two tastes, the loves you both have, and the words you both are.
- **Follow**, **Message** (opens a DM), **Share**, and a menu to **block** or **report** them.
- Their followers count.

## Who sees it

**Edit → Who sees your taste**: **Everyone**, **Friends** (people you follow who follow you back) or **Only me**. A friends-only profile still shows up in search, but only friends can open it; a private one never shows up anywhere. The same setting decides who can start a game about you. In a circle you're in, the daily drop and *Taste twins* can still use your loves.
