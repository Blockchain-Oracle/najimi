Open **Settings** from the settings button (the sliders) in the top bar on a phone, or at the bottom of the rail on a computer.

## Your profile

Name, bio, city, **Who sees your taste** (Everyone, Friends, Only me) and **Show me on the radar** (off until you turn it on).

## When Najimi acts for you

| Setting | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask me first** (default) | Before making a picture, Najimi shows a card with what it will make and what it uses of today's allowance. |
| **Just do it** | Pictures are made straight away (the daily allowance still applies). |
| **Only suggest** | Najimi never makes pictures; it describes what it would make instead. |

Whatever you choose, **a phone call and a post on X always wait for your yes**, and a hotel is only ever paid by you, in Nuitee's own sheet. In a circle, only the person who asked can say yes.

## Today

Meters for what you've used today, and what Najimi's thinking cost this month:

| | Guest | Signed in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages to Najimi | 30 | 100 |
| Pictures | 10 | 30 |
| Calls to venues | none | 3 |
| Rooms held | 2 | 6 |

Allowances reset every day (UTC). A failed picture, call or hold gives its allowance back, and **Try again** on an answer doesn't use one.

## Notifications

Najimi is an installable web app (iPhone: Share → *Add to Home Screen*; Android: **Install**). Turn notifications on here (on iPhone, add Najimi to your Home Screen first), in four groups:

- **Messages, follows and invites**
- **Games and daily drops**
- **Plans, bookings and business**
- **Your daily tune-up**

They only arrive when Najimi isn't open in front of you, and tapping one opens the right place. Circle chatter is gathered: at most one notice every ten minutes while you haven't read it.

## Languages

Najimi speaks **twelve languages**: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic (right to left), plus Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, Hindi, Chinese and Japanese in beta until a native speaker checks them. Najimi answers, and writes its cards, in the language you chose. Change it under **Language** at the top of Settings, or from the globe on a computer.
