Every conversation is a **room**: your own chat with Najimi, a circle, or a DM. They all work the same way and update live for everyone in them.

## The list

Open **Chats** from the history button at the top of any chat.

- **Tabs**: All, Najimi, Circles, People.
- **Pinned** rooms sit in a band of big faces at the top.
- **Unread** counts, and rows grouped by **Today**, **This week** and **Earlier**.
- **Search** by a chat's title, a circle's name or a person.
- Each row's menu: pin, and delete (your Najimi chats) or leave (circles and DMs). Leaving a circle or DM you started deletes it for everyone.

## Writing

The composer is one row: **+**, your message, the **mic**, and **send**.

- **+** adds up to four photos (from your library or the camera) and, in circles and DMs, starts a **poll**.
- The **mic** dictates in your language and fills in the box; nothing sends until you tap send.
- While Najimi answers, send becomes **stop**. What it already said stays.

## Najimi in a room

In your own chat Najimi answers every message. In a circle or DM, call it: `@najimi find us a spot tonight`, or start with "Najimi…". An empty circle offers three: a spot tonight, a film for all of us, music for all of us.

Its answers arrive as **cards**: picks you can open, a group fit with every member's face and fit, a night plan with its route, hotels with prices, a game, a poll, a call or a post waiting for your yes. Above the answer, a short **run** shows its Qloo lookups, web searches and questions as steps (*qloo.insights · place · Lagos → 5*). Under the latest answer: **Copy**, **Try again** and **Share this chat**. See [The agent](/docs/agent).

## Polls

Anyone in a circle or DM can start one (2 to 4 choices), and so can Najimi when the room can't decide; its choices carry the real artwork and open the thing. Everyone gets one vote and can change it, and the bars move live. Ask Najimi "what won?" and it reads the votes.

## Photos

Photos you share are re-encoded when they arrive, which strips their location and camera data, and they're stored privately. Najimi sees them and answers like a friend would; it only restyles one when you ask (see [Real-world actions](/docs/agent/actions#pictures)).

## Your own chat with Najimi

Tap the title to rename it. A new chat is titled for you after the first answer, in your language. Older messages load as you scroll up.
