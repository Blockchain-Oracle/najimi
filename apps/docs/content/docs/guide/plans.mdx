## A night out

Three stops in order, each from Qloo's places near the area you named, filtered for that part of the night:

| Stop | When | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drinks** | your start time | cocktail bars, bars, lounges |
| **Food** | 90 minutes later | restaurants |
| **Late** | 3½ hours after the start | night clubs, live music venues and live-music bars |

In a circle every stop is scored for **each person**, with their face and fit, from everyone's blended taste. The route is real (walking when the stops are close, driving across a city), and **shows that night** near the plan come with tickets.

## A trip

One to five days. Qloo's heatmap of the city for your taste picks a different area for each day, and each day gets a morning stop (a café, a museum, a gallery), an afternoon one (a park, a bookshop) and dinner, all routed.

## The plan page

Every plan has a link anyone can open: the route on a map with numbered stops, **Add to calendar** (Google, Outlook or a calendar file), **The whole route** in Google Maps, **Uber** for the whole night, an Uber for each leg, and per stop **Directions** and (signed in) **Ask Najimi to book**, which hands the stop to Najimi to phone. **Somewhere to stay** asks Najimi for hotels nearby.

## Hotels, booked for real

<Steps>
<Step>
**Ask.** "Somewhere to stay near Ikoyi Friday, two nights." Najimi shows real rooms and prices near there, best rated first.
</Step>
<Step>
**Book.** Tapping **Book** holds the price with Nuitee. Nothing is charged yet.
</Step>
<Step>
**Pay in Nuitee's own sheet.** Your details, then Nuitee's payment form. Nuitee is the merchant; Najimi never sees your card.
</Step>
<Step>
**Confirmed.** The booking lands in the chat as a card with the confirmation, **Add to calendar**, and **Cancel** while it's free to. If the hotel is slow to confirm, Najimi keeps checking and tells you.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tables, booked by phone

Ask Najimi to book a table and it shows how its AI caller will open the call. When you tap **Call**, it phones the venue, says it's an AI calling on your behalf and that the call is recorded, and asks for your table. The card's promise: Najimi never pays or agrees to a deposit. The answer comes back into the chat: booked (under your name), or the time they offered instead. Calls only go out between 8am and 9pm your time. See [Real-world actions](/docs/agent/actions#phone-calls).

## Afterwards

Najimi asks **How was it?**: three hours after a table, the morning after a hotel stay, twelve hours after a night out starts, and the day after a one- or two-day trip ends. Tell it you loved a place you booked and Najimi can add it to your taste. Nights, trips, stays and tables become stamps in your [Passport](/docs/guide/passport).
