Every game is up to five things, scored from 0 to 100 each, and every answer stays hidden until you've given yours. In *Guess my wavelength* and *Blind Taste*, **Qloo knows the answers**: it scores each thing against the real taste of the person it's about. In *Know me* and *Taste twins*, the answer is the person's own loves. **Najimi plays too**: in *Guess my wavelength*, *Know me* and *Taste twins* it's beatable; *Blind Taste* is you against Qloo's own prediction.

## The four games

<Accordions type="single" defaultValue="wavelength">
<Accordion title="Guess my wavelength" value="wavelength">
Five things, from obviously them to not at all. Swing the dial from *Not their thing* to *Obsessed*: how much would they love it? The closer to Qloo's number, the more you score (*Bullseye*, *So close*, *Not bad*, *Way off*). Najimi's guesses land 8 to 30 points off.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How well do you know me" value="knowme">
For each round, which of these did they pick as a love? One is real; the others are things from other people's tastes. Najimi gets about two in three right.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Taste twins" value="twins">
For circles: here's something only one of you loves. Who? Needs two or more people.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Blind Taste" value="blind">
Five things you probably don't know, picked for **you**. Swipe right for *Love it*, left for *Nope*, up for *Maybe*. Qloo has already predicted how you'll feel; your score is how often you agree. Only the person it was dealt for can play it.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Where to play

- **People → Play**: your wavelength, Blind Taste and *Know me?*, all about you, plus the **Najimi Lounge**.
- **In a circle or DM**: the gamepad at the top deals a game about any member, and shows the room's week.
- **Ask Najimi**: "let's play", "quiz us about Kemi". It deals the game and plays too.

Starting a game about someone else needs their taste to be public, or set to Friends and you to be friends. The daily drop and *Taste twins* use the loves of the people in the circle.

## Challenge anyone

Every round has its own link. Anyone can play it in their browser with no account: they type a name and go. (A *Blind Taste* is personal: only the person it was dealt for can play it.) Finished players land on the round's board beside Najimi, and in a circle a line like *Kemi scored 412 on Guess my wavelength · Najimi 380* lands in the chat. The person the game is about hears when someone plays it.

## Every morning

At 9am (the circle starter's time), Najimi deals **Today's drop** into every circle that talked in the last week: a *Guess my wavelength* about one member, taking turns. Everyone gets a notification.

## Scores

- **This week**: a rolling seven-day board, for a circle or for everyone with a public profile.
- **Streak**: days in a row you played the daily drop.
- **Qloo knows you**: how often Blind Taste predicted you right.
