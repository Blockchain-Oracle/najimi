**Najimi** (馴染み, *familiar*) is a taste copilot. Tell it three things you love (a book, a show, an artist, a place) and from then on anything you ask gets an answer that fits *you*.

It answers in two ways:

- **It recommends real things.** [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com) connects your loves to music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations and more. Every pick says why ("because you love Insecure").
- **It makes things.** It restyles your own photos, writes lighting plans an electrician can install, finds real products from brands that fit you, cooks recipes at the right texture, and makes short films, all shaped by the words your taste is made of.

It works for other people too: keep a taste for your mum, a partner or a client, and do any of the above for them.
