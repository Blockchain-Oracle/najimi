title: Find your people
description: What Najimi is, in one page.
---

**Najimi** (馴染み, *your regular place*) is a growth agent for places and consumer brands: restaurants, bars, cafés, venues, museums, galleries, hotels, shops, brands and artists.

You paste your website. Najimi asks [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com) who your people are: what else they love across music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands and places, and where they are. Then its agents propose the work that reaches them, and nothing goes out until you approve it.

These docs grow with the product: each page arrives with the feature it describes.
