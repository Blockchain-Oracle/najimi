---
title: The taste copilot
description: What Najimi is, in one page.
---

**Najimi** (馴染み, *familiar*) is a taste copilot: you tell it a few things you love — a book, a show, an artist, a place — and from then on anything you ask gets an answer that fits *you*.

It answers in two ways:

- **It recommends real things.** [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com) connects your loves to music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations and more. Every pick says why ("because you love Fleabag").
- **It makes things.** It restyles your photos, makes short videos, and writes lighting plans and recipes, all shaped by the words your taste is made of.

It works for other people too: keep a taste profile for your mum, a partner or a client, and do any of the above for them.

These docs grow with the product: each page arrives with the feature it describes.
