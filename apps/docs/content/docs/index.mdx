**Najimi** (馴染み, *someone you know well*) is a social app where your taste is your identity. Tell it a few things you love, or let it read what you already follow, and [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com)'s taste graph turns that into a taste that reaches music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations and people. Everything in the app is that taste, seen a different way.

## The three-minute tour

1. **Start as a guest.** Pick three things you love (or bring your Letterboxd, Last.fm or X handle). Your taste builds live.
2. **Your feed plays.** Song previews, trailers, places and podcasts, each with a *because* chip. Love or skip, and the next cards change.
3. **Earth.** Fly to your city and watch columns rise where your taste lives. Tap one to read its scene.
4. **People.** Join the Najimi Lounge, play *Guess my wavelength* against Najimi, and land on the leaderboard.
5. **"9pm, Lekki, we want to go out."** Najimi plans the night with a route, shows how well each stop fits each person, and books it.
6. **"I run a café in Yaba."** Najimi builds a plan: who your people are, where they cluster, what's rising, what to try.
7. **Your Passport.** A year of stamps, and a Wrapped story set that shares straight to Instagram.

## Try it

Open [Najimi](https://najimi.xyz) on your phone. No account is needed: you start as a guest, and signing in later keeps everything. Add it to your Home Screen for notifications.
