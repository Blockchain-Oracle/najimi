**Najimi** (馴染み, *someone you know well*) is a social app where your taste is your identity. Tell it a few things you love (an artist, a show, a book, a place) and [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com) turns them into a taste that reaches music, film, TV, books, podcasts, brands, places, destinations and people. Every pick says why ("because you love Insecure").

Najimi is being built out from that taste, one stage at a time: a feed of music and culture you can steer, the map of where your people are, circles where an AI agent plans and books with your friends, games that test how well you know each other, and a business mode. This site describes what is live today and grows with each stage.

## Try it

Open [najimi.xyz](https://najimi.xyz) on your phone. No account is needed: you start as a guest, and signing in later keeps everything.
